Payout Registration

Register your PIX key once so the CRF Anapu tree-planting support (R$ 5.00 per tree) can be paid directly to you. You only need to do this once.

Your PIX key is private. It is sent only to a restricted payout register — it is never written to the public DAO ledger. Please confirm the key carefully: a mistyped key means a payment that cannot be delivered. If you have no PIX key, leave it blank and tell us how you can be paid instead.
Use the same email you use on the SunMint planting app, so your trees and your payout match up.
Leave blank if you have no PIX key — see below.
If you are under 18 and the key belongs to a parent or guardian, enter their name.
