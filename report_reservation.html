Reservation Reporter

Record a reservation: a buyer has paid cash for a specific QR-coded item but has not yet collected it. The item is marked RESERVED and the cash is booked now — the inventory drop and the tree-planting liability happen later, at settlement.

The specific reserved item. It must already exist in the ledger (mint it first if un-serialised).
If the item is a gift, put the intended recipient here and note who paid below.
Used to email the buyer when the reservation is settled.
The DAO member who took the cash (cash custodian).
Evidence the money changed hands (photo/receipt).
